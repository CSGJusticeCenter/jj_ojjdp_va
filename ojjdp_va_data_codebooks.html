Virginia DJJ Data File Codebooks

Data Sample:

  • Juvenile intake complaints with intake dates from July 1, 2015 - June 30, 2021

  • Dispositions – all dispositions for the intake complaints in the sample.

  • Caseload – all intake complaints in the sample that are associated with a workload status for diversion - “Diversion At Intake” or “Diversion Program Beyond 90 Days”

For all missing data plots, here are the % missingness categories: “Good” <= 5% missing, “OK” <= 40% missing, “Bad” <= 80% missing, and “Remove” = 100% missing


This syntax pulls in all data files provided by DJJ stored as a series of Excel file tabs – creates high-level codebooks for each using the DataExplorer package (https://boxuancui.github.io/DataExplorer/index.html). File names reflect the names provided by Dallas County.

Intake Complaints, Intake Decision Descriptions

Intake Decision Intake Decision Description Prelim Intake Decision Grouping (for analysis)
PETITION FILED A complaint was received, probable cause was established, and the offense decision was to file a petition. Petitioned
PETITION/DETENTION ORDER FILED A complaint was received, probable cause was established, and the offense decision was to file a petition and issue a detention order. Petitioned
DETENTION ORDER ONLY Detention order only issued for pre-dispositional cases. Other
COURT SUMMONS Law enforcement files a court summons directly with the court (e.g., traffic offenses, low-level alcohol/marijuana offenses). In some CSUs, the summons is then entered as a complaint using this offense decision. In other CSUs, the CSU never sees the summons. Petitioned
PETITION/SHELTER CARE FILED A complaint/affidavit was received and probable cause established. The juvenile requires placement outside the home and secure detention is not appropriate. Petitioned
UNSUCCESSFUL DIVERSION/PETITION FILED Offense decision on original complaint was to divert. Diversion contract was signed and juvenile violates diversion contract and decision was made (with supervisor approval) to void the diversion and file a petition on the offense. Diverted
RESOLVED A complaint was received and the issue was resolved at intake. Resolved/Dismissed
SUCCESSFUL DIVERSION The offense decision on an original complaint was to divert. Juvenile successfully completes all obligations of the diversion contract and the intake was closed. Diverted
COMPLAINT UNFOUNDED A complaint was received and probable cause could not be established. Resolved/Dismissed
UNSUCCESSFUL DIVERSION/NO PETITION FILED The offense decision on an original complaint was to divert. The diversion contract was signed and juvenile violates diversion contract; however, a petition was not filed. Diverted
REFERRED TO ANOTHER AGY FOR SV A complaint was received, probable cause was established, and the family was referred to another agency for services. Diverted (Informal)
UNOFFICIAL COUNSELING-FAMILY C A complaint was received and the intake officer recommended family counseling to the clients. Follow up by the intake officer is not required. Diverted (Informal)
ACCEPTED VIA ICJ Juvenile was accepted from another state for supervision. Other
RETURNED TO PROBATION SUPERVIS A complaint was received, probable cause was established, and the juvenile was already on probation for another offense. The juvenile will remain on probation and no new action will be taken. Other
PENDING A complaint was received and probable cause established. This code is for short-term use only. Generally, this is used when the intention is to divert and are awaiting the diversion appointment or when waiting for more information from the police department. Other
SHELTER CARE ONLY The juvenile was being sent to a non-secure facility or emergency shelter specifically approved to provide a range of as-needed services on an individual basis. See § 16.1-248.1 of the Code of Virginia for additional information. Other
RETURNED TO OUT-OF-STATE A complaint was received, the juvenile was under supervision in Virginia from another state, and the juvenile was returned to that state. Other
REQ'D TO PARTICIPATE-DIVERSION A complaint was received, probable cause was established, and the complaint was diverted. Diversion is the handling of a juvenile intake compliant in an informal manner rather than through the official court process. The intake officer must develop a plan for the juvenile that may include counseling, informal supervision, restitution, community service, or other programs. The juvenile and his/her parents must agree to the diversion plan. Informal supervision is limited to 90 days for truancy and 120 days for all other offenses. The following complaints may not be diverted: an alleged violent juvenile felony, a complaint after a prior diversion or adjudication on a felony offense, and a second or subsequent truancy complaint. See § 16.1-227 and 16.1- 260 of the Code of Virginia for additional information. Diverted
